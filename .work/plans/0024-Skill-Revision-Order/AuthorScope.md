- **Infer the brief.** Start from the likely reader, purpose, and destination using available context. State assumptions that could change the result.
- **Use the project's voice.** Follow the nearest applicable writing and style guidance by default. Depart when the audience or purpose calls for it.
- **Revise for coherence within scope.** A broad revision can change structure and tone across the artifact. Preserve established facts and commitments unless the human authorizes changing them.
- **Verify or qualify material claims.** Retain a useful claim when its evidence limit is clear. Cite supporting evidence where the reader needs it.
- **Deliver the artifact and material limits.** Give the text or its location. Include checks and gaps when they affect use. Publishing or sending continues to require authorization.
