# Design: scope for revision 14

**Status:** Approved and implemented. See [Progress](Progress.md) for verification.

Keep [Design](../../../skills/design/SKILL.md) as the owner of visual direction and production whenever the requested outcome changes how the work looks, feels, or communicates. This includes authorized implementation and the interface concept branch absorbed from Imagine. This revision kept Critique as the review owner; the later [Critique merge](CritiqueScope.md) moved visual review into Design.

- **Agree on the effect and identity boundaries.** The human and Design agree on the intended effect and what the identity must preserve. Within those boundaries, Design chooses composition, visual language, and execution.
- **Start with the project's own material.** Look outside only when comparison could resolve an open direction or pattern. When direction is open, show two or three materially different approaches before developing one. For a scoped refinement, show one approach within the established direction.
- **Require a live baseline for redesign.** Inspect the affected interface in its runtime before redesigning it. If it cannot be viewed, pause the redesign. Preserve working behavior and established identity by default. Ask before altering interactions, access, content meaning, or distinctive identity unless the change is already authorized.
- **Translate a reference by intent.** Carry over hierarchy, tone, and relationships. Match literal details when the requester explicitly makes them part of the target. Preserve project constraints and identify material departures from an agreed target.
- **Agree before generating a concept.** Confirm the concept brief's direction, content, and constraints explicitly. Reuse agreement on the same brief. If that brief also authorizes implementation, proceed with a generated direction that fits; ask if adopting it changes direction or scope. Permission to generate alone does not authorize implementation.
- **Use Critique for substantial visual work.** Inspect the result in its intended medium first, then give Critique the brief and the actual preview or export. Recheck material corrections before delivery. Keep behavioral verification with Test when it needs distinct evidence.

The entry point, direction and reference guidance, review handoff, and affected checks reflect these choices. The concept branch remains part of Design.
