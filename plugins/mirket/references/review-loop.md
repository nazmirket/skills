# Resolve findings and recheck

Use after [Audit](../skills/audit/SKILL.md), [Critique](../skills/critique/SKILL.md), or a substantial draft [Plan](../skills/plan/SKILL.md) has material findings. Keep the calling workflow as the owner. Review-only authorization ends with a report; use this loop to change the artifact only when correction is in scope.

1. Record the fixed artifact or baseline, applicable criteria, and findings. For each material finding capture the location or condition, evidence, impact, and a concrete next step. Separate findings from missing information and preferences.
2. Decide each material finding: fix, rebut with evidence, or record the tradeoff for the human. Reuse the independent review of this exact target if one already exists. If independence is required and has not been established, commission a fresh reviewer who did not author the artifact, where authorized and available; otherwise report the gate as unmet.
3. Repair the smallest coherent batch. Recheck impacted criteria with fresh evidence and send material repairs back to the independent reviewer where required. Reuse unchanged evidence; a code edit, screenshot or claim of completion is not verification by itself.
4. Stop when material findings are resolved with evidence, or when further work needs a decision or unavailable capability. Report the remaining findings and limits. Do not add an unreviewed polish change after the final check.
