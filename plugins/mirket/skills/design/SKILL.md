# Design

Deliver a visual direction, design artifact or working implementation that fits the audience, task and medium. Use [Critique](../critique/SKILL.md) for a quality review alone or for the rendered review of completed visual work.

## Orient and choose a route

1. Identify the audience, task, medium, deliverable and constraints. Inspect the product, real content, assets and conventions. Preserve earlier decisions and distinguish an approved visual target from inspiration.
2. Choose the route that fits the requested outcome. For a redesign, first view and capture the [live baseline](references/direction-and-composition.md#capture-the-redesign-baseline) and identify what must survive. If the existing interface cannot be viewed, stop the redesign until the baseline is visible and report the blocker. For an existing screenshot or mockup to reproduce, go straight to [reference fidelity](references/reference-fidelity.md) using the supplied image. Transfer a generated **interface** reference to [Imagine](../imagine/SKILL.md); it invokes the delivery steps below only for authorized implementation. For a generated poster, identity board, or other non-interface reference, keep ownership in Design and use [visual artifacts and assets](references/artifacts-and-assets.md) with the relevant host workflow. Use the [prototype method](../../references/prototype.md) when the deliverable is a disposable comparison to resolve a UI decision.
3. For a new or materially changed direction, use [direction and composition](references/direction-and-composition.md) to compare relevant examples and propose a representative direction. Use [Curate](../curate/SKILL.md) if the human needs a sourced comparison of real options. For a scoped refinement, keep the established direction and load only the references relevant to the change.

## Develop and deliver

4. Resolve consequential choices before expanding the work. Show a representative slice using real content and important states if that will resolve an unsettled direction. Ask the human when a consequential direction remains unresolved; honor existing authorization and reuse prior decisions rather than asking again. State material assumptions when authorized to choose.
5. Produce the requested artifact or implementation using the existing stack, approved assets and actual content. Keep working renders and copied references in `.work/temp/<task>/` under the [workspace rules](../../references/workspace.md). Keep real interactions and relevant states working; apply brand, accessibility and responsive constraints. Label unresolved content and assumptions. For a visual reproduction, preserve observed structure and record intentional departures rather than substituting a new style.
6. Hand the rendered quality review to [Critique](../critique/SKILL.md) with the approved target, constraints, affected sizes and states, and any live baseline. Reuse that review and its recheck of material corrections. Use [Test](../test/SKILL.md) for focused behavior evidence where needed; use the [prototype method](../../references/prototype.md) to record what a disposable comparison actually showed.
7. Deliver the artifact, key decisions, evidence and material gaps. Report any missing reference, inaccessible view or uninspected rendering. A captured screenshot or source file alone cannot establish visual quality.

**Done:** The requested direction, artifact or implementation is delivered with its decisions and evidence. Report any unverified condition or unanswered prototype question.
