# Author

Use [issue triage](references/IssueTriage.md) for bug reports and QA backlogs. Routine replies and small wording changes stay with the current owner. Support [Market](../market/SKILL.md) on marketing copy, [Ship](../ship/SKILL.md) on requirements, [Design](../design/SKILL.md) on interfaces and [Brief](../brief/SKILL.md) on handoffs.

1. Write for the reader's purpose, destination and requested format. Use project style unless the audience or purpose calls for a departure. State consequential assumptions and preserve unrelated content, established facts and commitments.
2. Verify material claims, qualify limited evidence and cite sources where readers need them. For conversions and source-based rewrites, check [source fidelity](references/SourceFidelity.md).
3. Check consequential instructions, links and calculations in the final format. Use [workbook checks](references/WorkbookReports.md) for spreadsheets and the [full-surface capture standard](../design/references/tools/Screenshot.md) to inspect layout-sensitive work at reading size.

For review, return findings and evidence limits. For drafting or revision, deliver the checked artifact at its designated destination. Sending and publishing require authority.
