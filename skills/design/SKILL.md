# Design

Design owns visual artifacts and interface interactions. Agree on the intended effect and what the identity must preserve. Choose composition, visual language and execution within that brief. Inspect the result in its intended medium.

**Every critique and visual check requires a verified, high-resolution capture of the complete reviewed surface.** For web work, capture the full page at the intended CSS viewport and at least 2× native pixel density. Check the saved original against the measured surface and inspect it at actual pixels. Crops and viewport shots supply detail. If full evidence is unavailable, report the missing coverage and leave visual acceptance open. Apply the [capture standard](references/tools/Screenshot.md), including for before/after comparisons and non-web work.

| Agree what the design must achieve and preserve | [Author Brief](references/flows/AuthorBrief.md) |

Add a flow only for a distinct result. Use [formats](references/formats/README.md) for acceptance and handoff, [concepts](references/concepts/README.md) for craft, and [styles](references/styles/README.md) to choose a treatment that serves the brief. For interaction findings, exercise the requested input method and observe the outcome. Keep temporary evidence in the project's ignored scratch area and deliver requested artifacts to their designated destination.
