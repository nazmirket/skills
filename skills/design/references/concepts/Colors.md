# Colors

Use to establish palette roles, emphasis, tone, and states across the surfaces the audience will see.

## Give color a job

Begin with foreground and surface pairs from the approved identity. Assign accent, action, focus, feedback and data roles as needed. On a busy screen, reserve the strongest difference in value or saturation for the decision that matters now. A brand accent shared by a decorative label, error and primary control needs additional cues to separate their jobs.

## Verify pairs and states

Check text and focus against their actual surfaces, including supported themes and variable media. Sample the least legible region under translucent text or controls. Use a stable backing when a moving crop can invalidate the pair. Apply the [contrast and input checks](Accessibility.md) to the result.

Place a selected item, an error and an ordinary item side by side. Cover the color and see whether position, outlined focus, words or shape still distinguish them. Tune each foreground and surface pair in dark mode and check the same task again, including hover, disabled and selected states.
