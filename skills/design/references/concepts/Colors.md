# Colors

Use to establish palette roles, emphasis, tone, and states across the surfaces the audience will see.

## Give color a job

Set roles for foreground, background, surfaces, action, focus, feedback and data. Establish actual foreground and background pairs, including layers, images, hover, disabled and selected states. Check relevant contrast against [accessibility needs](Accessibility.md); distinguish state through words, shape or position as well as hue. Keep color reserved for the level of emphasis it earns. A dark theme needs tuned pairs and surface relationships rather than mechanically inverted colors.

## Verify pairs and states

Compare representative surfaces in context, including hover, focus, disabled, selection, errors and data series where relevant. Test actual foreground and background pairs in supported themes and with the media they may overlay. Use position, wording or shape alongside hue when someone must distinguish states.
