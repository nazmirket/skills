# Icons

Use when a compact mark must convey an action, category, state or identity.

## Keep the family consistent

Draw and inspect at intended size. Use compatible stroke, fills, terminals, corners, contrast and optical weight. Simplify details that merge at small sizes. Use labels where the symbol is unfamiliar or the consequence matters.

## Check meaning and use

Give icon-only controls an accessible name and hide decorative marks from assistive technology where appropriate. Check active and disabled states, touch target, bidirectional meaning and neighboring symbols. Choose editable [vector](Vector.md) sources when they make the glyph easier to maintain.
