# Images

Use to select, capture, direct or prepare photography, illustrations and raster assets.

## Choose the subject and crop

Use actual product material, photography, illustration or data where it explains something. Select a crop that preserves the subject, scale and context, then check it at the final ratios. Protect text readability when it overlays media; reserve space for loaded imagery and choose a meaningful fallback when it fails. Do not represent a generated stand-in as a real product view, customer or result.

## Prepare the actual asset

Check permission and provenance; preserve editable sources where useful. Inspect actual dimensions, crop, density, file weight, color and transparency. For print, confirm finished size, bleed, safe margins, resolution and supplier requirements. For responsive web assets, reserve space and choose a fitting crop and loading treatment. Give informative images a purpose-specific text alternative.
