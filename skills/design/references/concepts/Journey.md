# Journeys

Use to understand or improve a goal that spans steps, roles, channels or interruptions. Keep observed current paths separate from proposed ones.

## Map the decision points

For each meaningful step, record the situation, what the person knows, action, feedback, dependency and next step. Capture waiting, switching channels and recovery as well as the happy path. Attach evidence to each claimed point of friction and mark assumptions.

## Change the part that matters

Find avoidable decisions, repeated work, missing handoffs or points with no clear status. Prioritize by effect on the real goal. Preserve handoff ownership and the ability to pick up later. Use [UX](UX.md) for the local state and interaction design of a step.
