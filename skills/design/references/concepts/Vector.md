# Vector artwork

Use for marks, diagrams, line art and assets that need clean scaling or editable shape geometry.

## Build editable geometry

Set a useful artboard and view box. Use intentional paths, fills, strokes and joins. Prefer simpler geometry that preserves the silhouette and keep editable text or its source where required. Align critical edges by how they render at target scale; an enlarged canvas can hide fine-detail problems.

## Verify export and reuse

Inspect the smallest expected size, the largest use, typical backgrounds and any dark or single-color variants. Confirm bounds, clipping, embedded fonts or images, and that the export opens in the intended consumer. Give the recipient native source and an appropriate delivery format.
