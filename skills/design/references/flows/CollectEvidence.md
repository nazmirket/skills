# Collect design evidence

Use to capture what is observable about a particular visual asset, interface or workflow. Establish the target, decision it will inform, and relevant viewing conditions. Gather only the evidence that could change that decision.

Open a [tool guide](../tools/README.md) when the human asks for its guidance or you need its instructions. Read it before giving a procedure or attempting the operation. A Figma handoff procedure routes to [Figma](../tools/Figma.md). A request to use Mirket's screenshot guidance routes to [Screenshot](../tools/Screenshot.md) before the first capture attempt, even if a ready capture tool is available. A requested PNG or a tool named only as the source of sufficient evidence does not by itself require a guide.

- Open the actual artifact or runtime. Record its location or version, entry point, relevant states, input, viewport or physical size, and how to reproduce the view. Capture representative before states and the output a person actually sees. Mark supplied screenshots, reports and inferred behavior separately from direct observations.
- When collecting screenshots for a review, cover the whole scope at a resolution the reviewer can read. For a complete page, use a full-page view or ordered sections and add detailed views for findings the overview cannot show clearly.
- Inventory the project's available content, approved assets, brand cues, interaction conventions and known constraints. Record where material references and assets came from and what they may be used for. Label stale, unavailable or unverified material.
- For a redesign, view the affected interface live before changes. Capture primary paths, feedback, navigation and important error or access states. Source and captures alone cannot satisfy the live baseline. If runtime access is missing, report the blocker and leave the baseline incomplete.
- When content may break the interface, inspect the model or schema to identify valid lengths, counts, missing values and locale conditions. Use realistic normal and adverse inputs in the actual rendering path with isolated fixtures or a safe local override. Keep test data and overrides out of production. Note when you could not reach the real path.

Return the visible baseline, traceable evidence and gaps, or the procedure if that was requested. Stop before interpreting what the design should become or editing the target.
