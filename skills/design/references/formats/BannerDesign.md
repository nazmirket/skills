# Banner design

Use for a banner, channel header, campaign placement, or large-format sign whose crop, surrounding space, or finish governs the design. Use [Canvas Design](CanvasDesign.md) for static pieces without these placement constraints, or [Web Design](WebDesign.md) for a live interface region with its own interactions.

## Inventory the placements

Name the audience, viewing distance or context, main message, required copy and marks, and the destination for each version. Use the current placement spec or the print supplier's actual template. Record the dimensions and aspect ratio, visible area or trim, safe area, known overlays or hardware, and output requirements. Share an editable master where useful; proof each required placement.

| Placement | Decide before building |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed digital banner or device-cropped header | Where the image is cropped, what the host covers, what must remain visible, and whether the host provides a separate title or action. |
| Responsive ad built from separate assets | Which copy, image, and marks the host assembles. Review allowed combinations and keep each asset readable or meaningful on its own. |
| Printed or mounted banner | Finished dimensions and scale, material, viewing distance, edges or seams, hems, grommets or pockets, and the supplier's bleed, color, resolution and export requirements. |

Proof a cropped channel header with the identity and title inside the common visible area. For an ad assembled from separate inputs, vary the headline and image independently. On a mounted print, overlay the actual trim and fastening template on the master and inspect the closest text or mark.

## Build and proof each useful variant

Set one priority and place essential copy and marks where they survive the most constrained view. Keep the message, brand cues and factual content stable while varying crop, scale or arrangement. For a large change in shape, recompose before shrinking type. For print, keep critical content away from trim and finishing hardware; extend backgrounds into bleed only as specified.

Open the actual exports. Preview digital placements with the real overlay or crop where available, including the smallest or most obstructed view. Check dimensions, file weight and legibility there. For print, inspect trim, safe areas, hardware clearance, effective raster detail and the supplier proof if available. A screen mockup cannot establish the finished color, material, or installation.

Return the editable master and the requested exports named by placement, with the spec or template used and any material limits. Preparing files does not authorize uploading, printing or publishing them.
