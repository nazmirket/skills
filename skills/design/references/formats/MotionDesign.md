# Motion design

Use for a motion deliverable or a distinct motion task: a branded sequence, animated asset, loop, or interaction whose timing and coordination are the result. Use [Motion](../concepts/Motion.md) for an ordinary transition, [Gestures](../concepts/Gestures.md) for direct manipulation, and [Video](../concepts/Video.md) for narrative editing, sound and captions.

## Define what changes over time

Name what should become clearer or felt through motion, who sees it, and where it plays. Specify the trigger or first beat, the focal element, what follows and why, and the last state. Choose character through weight, pace and overlap. A still or direct state change is the comparison when the motion's purpose is uncertain.

Storyboard the key beats or prototype the important transition before multiplying it. Specify target size, playback or interaction context, duration, first and final frame, and loop or end behavior. For interactive work, identify who controls playback and what happens on repeat, interruption, reversal or reduced motion. Keep underlying state and access intact if playback is skipped. For standalone motion, keep essential information available in an appropriate still or textual alternative when needed.

## Verify in playback

- **Read at speed.** Play the actual delivered asset in the intended player, at target size and speed. Check attention order, time to read, crop and the ending or loop seam. Inspect individual frames to diagnose a problem and replay at speed to judge the fix.
- **Try the case that could break it.** For interactive motion, try repeated and rapid activation, cancel or reverse where relevant, and the intended reduced-motion path. For a rendered sequence, inspect the actual first and last frames, transparency or background, and any supplied poster. For example, a panel that reopens during its exit must remain open after the old exit finishes.
- **Test the delivery mechanism.** Verify duration, timing and rendering in the actual consumer. Check feature support of the chosen player for a vector animation, and measure on representative hardware before claiming smoothness or speed. Use [Test](../../../test/SKILL.md) for repeatable behavioral or performance evidence. A few keyframes or a still cannot establish playback.

Deliver the agreed editable source or animation specification and export, with dimensions, duration, loop behavior, and fallback as applicable. Report which player, preferences, or hardware you checked and what remains untested.
