# Baroque

Use drama, motion and controlled abundance for a piece that calls for spectacle or ritual.

## Build the visual language

Build an unmistakable lead through light and dark, diagonal movement, scale and framing. Concentrate ornament near focal transitions and contrast it with quiet areas. Pair elaborate detail with clean locations for reading and action.

## Avoid the cliché

Avoid spreading equal ornament everywhere or treating generic flourishes as historical accuracy. Too many focal points undo the drama.

## Check it in use

View at final size and as a thumbnail. Confirm the subject and reading path survive after fine ornament drops away.

Follow the outstretched arms and directional light in Caravaggio’s *The Supper at Emmaus*. Sketch only the largest gestures and the main light source. Check whether the moment of recognition still has a clear focus.
