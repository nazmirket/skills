# Bohemian

Use a layered, handmade feel when the subject and materials support informality and individual expression.

## Build the visual language

Work with warm or earthy anchors, varied textiles or textures, organic composition and purposeful mixed patterns. Choose one dominant pattern family and one supporting contrast. Where motifs reference a culture, identify their origin and use authentic or authorized material.

## Avoid the cliché

Avoid a collection of generic cultural motifs, mismatched decoration with no hierarchy, and rough texture behind the information people need.

## Check it in use

Check whether the palette and patterns still feel related across calm and dense areas; choose one visual thread someone could recognize without decoration.
