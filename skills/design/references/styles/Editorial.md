# Editorial

Use an editorial treatment when people read, browse or follow an argument. Identify the lead story, the evidence that supports it and which related pieces someone might choose next.

Give the lead title and image an intentional relationship. A caption can locate the subject, a pull quote can mark a turn in the story and a rule can separate articles. Keep article text at a readable measure. Use a quieter, consistent rhythm for the index. Recompose the lead, caption and index together at the narrow width.

**Try it.** Lay out a curator's essay and three object records. Let a detail from one object introduce the essay, attach its actual caption and set the three records so their titles and dates line up. Try the longest title and a record with no image. Check that a reader can find both the start of the essay and the second object by name.
