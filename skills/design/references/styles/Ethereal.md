# Ethereal

Use delicacy and atmosphere when the work calls for wonder, calm, or an impression of lightness.

## Build the visual language

Create depth through soft light, diffuse boundaries, careful overlap and a narrow tonal range. Ground the composition with one clear point of focus and a steady treatment for text or controls.

## Avoid the cliché

Avoid low-contrast text, atmospheric effects on every edge, and movement that makes content drift out of reach. Lightness needs an anchor.

## Check it in use

Check actual output on dim and bright backgrounds, small sizes, and reduced motion if atmosphere is animated.

Compose a night-garden invitation with three soft value fields and one defined silhouette. Place the reading text on a calm field. Turn down brightness and reduce the image to a thumbnail, then check that the silhouette and event name remain readable.
