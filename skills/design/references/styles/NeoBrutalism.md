# Neo-Brutalism

Use bold, playful physicality when the brand can carry a loud, direct character.

## Build the visual language

Build on a highly legible grid; use strong outlines, flat fills, decisive type and hard offset shadows for selected surfaces. Reserve the loudest treatment for primary content or controls and give quiet regions room.

## Avoid the cliché

Avoid putting a border, bright fill and shadow on every element. Keep decorative offsets distinct from focus, selection and press feedback, and leave room for long labels.

## Check it in use

Compare ordinary and pressed controls, verify visible focus and check a narrow screen with realistic labels.

Build one filter control with a hard offset shadow. Inspect it at rest, on keyboard focus, when selected and during a press. The offset can establish depth while a separate indicator identifies focus. Check that long labels and adjacent controls leave room for both.
