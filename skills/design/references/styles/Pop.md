# Pop style

Use graphic punch and mass-media energy when the message benefits from bold recognition and repetition.

## Build the visual language

Choose a strong subject, simplified shapes, deliberate saturated color relationships and large decisive type. Use pattern, repeat, or a halftone as a directed visual device rather than a filler texture.

## Avoid the cliché

Avoid relying on unrelated loud elements or borrowing a recognizable work, character or brand mark without the right to use it. Make emphasis survive the saturated palette.

## Check it in use

Test at thumbnail and full size. Check that the intended headline, subject or action reads immediately and that fine textures reproduce cleanly.

Repeat the outline of a tea tin across a three-item comparison. Give each variety an original pictogram and a legible name. Cover the color fills and check whether the differences survive before introducing extra pattern.
