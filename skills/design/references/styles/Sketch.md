# Sketch style

Use visible construction marks and loose strokes to convey inquiry, craft or a work in progress.

## Build the visual language

Vary line weight, keep a clear gesture or construction underneath the drawing, and use selective detail to guide reading. Pair a rough drawing with reliable typesetting for essential explanations.

## Avoid the cliché

Avoid filtering every finished element into a sketch or suggesting an unconfirmed design is settled. Preserve confidence about actual affordances and status.

## Check it in use

Check the smaller scale and viewing distance: keep the important landmarks recognizable and any functional labels legible.

Look at how a drawing and its neighboring notes share a page in Leonardo da Vinci’s notebooks. For a process sketch, use a construction line to explain a real relationship and a short annotation to name what is being tested.
