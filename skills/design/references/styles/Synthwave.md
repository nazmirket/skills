# Synthwave

Use retrofuturist contrast to suggest nighttime, speed, music or an imagined analog future when the brief supports it.

## Build the visual language

Use a dark base, a small set of glowing accents and a clear motif such as a horizon, scanline texture or long perspective. Pair distinctive display type with readable supporting text. Make the accent hierarchy explicit.

## Avoid the cliché

Avoid neon outlines around everything, glare around small text and animated loops that compete with the user task. Use a static or reduced-motion expression where needed.

## Check it in use

Check contrast outside the glow, repeated viewing, and a subdued device or print treatment. Verify the silhouette reads without relying on the effects.

Study the cover of Kavinsky’s *OutRun* at album and thumbnail size. Identify which silhouette, light source and title treatment carry the mood after fine detail disappears. Use those relationships to compose a different subject. [Record Makers album artwork](https://recordmakers.com/album/outrun/)
