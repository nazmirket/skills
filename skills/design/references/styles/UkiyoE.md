# Ukiyo-e woodblock composition

Study how cropped foreground shapes, overlapping planes and flat areas of color can place a distant subject. For an original image of a river crossing, let a nearby reed bend into the frame, keep a boat in the middle distance and leave an open channel to the opposite bank. Test how the spacing and sizes of those shapes change perceived depth.

Using the brief's own subject, sketch two silhouettes with the same foreground and distant landmark. Change only their scale or overlap, then compare both at thumbnail size. Introduce a restricted palette and contour detail once the focal relationship reads at that size. Keep the study's provenance with the design.
