# Ukiyo-e woodblock composition

In Hokusai’s *Under the Wave off Kanagawa*, a nearby crest frames the distant triangular outline of Mount Fuji. Overlap, relative scale and the open shape between them establish depth. Use this particular print to study those decisions within a much wider print tradition. [The Met collection object](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/45434) · [The Met on woodblock printing](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/woodblock-prints-in-the-ukiyo-e-style)

Using the brief's own subject, sketch two silhouettes with the same foreground and distant landmark. Change only their scale or overlap, then compare both at thumbnail size. Introduce a restricted palette and contour detail once the focal relationship reads at that size. Keep the study's provenance with the design.
