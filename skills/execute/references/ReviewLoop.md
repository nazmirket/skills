# Resolve findings and recheck

Use for material findings on a fixed artifact from [Audit](../../audit/SKILL.md), [visual critique](../../design/references/flows/Critique.md), [Author](../../author/SKILL.md) or [Plan](../../plan/SKILL.md). Review-only work ends at findings. The task owner handles authorized corrections.

1. Reuse the review's target, criteria and evidence. For each material finding, fix it, rebut it with evidence, or bring the tradeoff to the human.
2. Batch related fixes and recheck affected criteria. Reuse evidence whose dependencies still hold. For [required independence](../../audit/SKILL.md#independent-review), reuse valid independent evidence or obtain an authorized independent review. Have the reviewer verify material repairs. Leave unmet gates open and continue unaffected work.
3. Close each finding when evidence meets its criteria. Reopen it only if a relevant premise changes. For a repeated finding, try a different in-scope repair or check, or report the blocker. Finish once findings and required gates are resolved or clearly reported as open.
