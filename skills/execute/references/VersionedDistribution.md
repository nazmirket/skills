# Prepare and publish a versioned artifact

The task owner prepares the candidate, records readiness, and publishes when authorized. Follow the project's release conventions and keep preparation, publication, and environment activation separate.

1. **Identify the candidate.** Pin the version, audience, channel, and source revision. Account for uncommitted and generated inputs; do not present a local snapshot as an exact committed version. Build through the established path. Record the toolchain and artifact identity, including a checksum when useful. Inspect what actually ships for missing files, required notices, secrets, and private or development-only material. Keep staging copies under ignored `.work/temp/` and put the deliverable in its designated location.
2. **Establish readiness.** Check version consistency and supported compatibility. Exercise representative installation or consumption of the exact distributable and check supported upgrades, including retained settings and data where relevant. Use [Test](../../test/SKILL.md) to fill evidence gaps. For plugins, distinguish discovery and installation from hook trust and activation. Give the fixed candidate to [Audit](../../audit/SKILL.md) when review is needed; keep its existing independent review rules. Record unresolved checks and limits. Leave readiness blocked when a required condition is unmet, and preserve the candidate with the reason. Include release notes and relevant upgrade or recovery instructions. Use [Migrate](../../migrate/SKILL.md) for substantive state transitions.
3. **Publish and verify only when authorized.** A preparation request ends with the candidate, notes, and readiness evidence. Publish only the identified artifact through the established channel. Resolve an ambiguous attempt before retrying and preserve immutable versions. Verify the published version and artifact identity from the consumer side.
4. **Hand off separately authorized activation.** Give [Deploy](../../deploy/SKILL.md) the exact artifact, target constraints, compatibility, relevant checks, and recovery path. Use its [operations branch](../../deploy/SKILL.md#operate-and-recover) for continued service health. Report preparation, local installation, publication, and activation as distinct results.
