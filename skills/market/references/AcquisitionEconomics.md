# Acquisition economics

Use when the decision is where to invest the next unit of acquisition effort or budget. Define the customer, conversion and decision horizon. Match cost and customer cohorts across the time it takes a prospect to buy. Separate paid, partner, sales-assisted and organic routes when their costs or buying cycles differ.

1. Trace one route from the audience's trigger through the channel, destination, qualified action, purchase and first useful outcome. Check whether the channel can reach the intended segment and whether sales, onboarding or support can serve the volume.
2. Count unique new paying customers. State what cost includes, such as media, creative, labor, referral incentives or partner fees. Keep existing-account expansion separate. Label media cost per attributed customer when attribution is all the data supports.
3. Estimate monthly contribution per paying customer from net revenue less relevant variable delivery costs. For stable contribution and retention, acquisition cost per customer divided by monthly contribution per customer estimates payback in months. For cash recovery, follow the cohort's actual receipts, refunds and variable cash costs over time, especially with upfront billing or steep early churn.
4. Compare the next affordable increment of spend or effort with its alternative. State the budget cap, cohort maturity date, minimum contribution or maximum payback period, downstream quality check and stop condition. Historical averages can hide saturation. Use a range for uncertain reach or conversion.

**Worked example.** A pilot spends $4,800 on placement and $1,200 on directly attributable sales work. Twelve new customers are credited to it. Media cost per attributed customer is $400. Including sales work gives $500 per customer. At a steady $100 monthly contribution per retained customer, simple payback is five months. Recheck the cohort's retention and whether those customers would have arrived anyway before recommending expansion.

Return a keep, change, pause or bounded test recommendation with the assumptions that could reverse it. Use [Measurement](Measurement.md) if incrementality or event quality drives the decision. Spend and outreach stay within existing authority.
