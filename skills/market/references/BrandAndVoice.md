# Brand and voice

Use to define how the brand behaves in language or bring inconsistent work into line. Start from the approved positioning, distinctive identity, current product experience, and examples people have actually seen. Label draft guidance until the human settles any change to identity or claims.

## Turn identity into editing decisions

- Collect a few approved samples from contrasting surfaces and actual customer phrasing. Identify repeatable choices in vocabulary, sentence rhythm, technical depth and point of view. Check whether a repeated habit serves the audience or just leaked from one author.
- Write three or four traits only if each changes a sentence. Pair each with what to do, where to dial it back, a counterexample, and a repaired line. For example, “practical” means naming the next step; “Don't worry, it's easy” does neither that nor useful reassurance.
- Separate stable voice from situational tone. For a discovery page, explain the value in the audience's terms. In onboarding, name the action and result. In an incident, lead with impact and current status; remove humor and unverified certainty. In billing, state amounts, terms, and dates plainly.
- Build a short lexicon of preferred product names, audience terms, avoided jargon, and claim limits. Keep localization and inclusive language in view: idioms, cultural references, and humor do not travel unchanged. Do not invent a founder quote, testimonial, guarantee, or personality based only on competitors.

## Verify and hand off

Apply the guide to a real headline, product instruction, and sensitive message; check that the outputs sound related without forcing the same tone. Hand off approved traits, examples, vocabulary, explicit exceptions and source samples. Use Design for the visual identity and keep naming or positioning decisions traceable.

When a voice guide will be used across regions, give the local writer the intended effect and the terms that carry a product commitment. Have them explain what a reader would expect after seeing their wording in context. Route localized campaign copy to [Messaging and copy](MessagingAndCopy.md#adapt-an-approved-message).
