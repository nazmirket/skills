# Campaigns and channels

Use for a campaign, go-to-market communication, or distribution plan. Write the outcome as a change among a defined audience, not a list of posts. Check product readiness with Ship before committing to availability, eligibility, dates or reach.

| Decision | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Audience and offer | Segment, trigger, promise, supporting proof, and intended action |
| Channel fit | Where that audience already pays attention, why the message fits, delivery constraints and cost |
| Sequence | What primes, announces and follows up; destination, owner, dependency, and review status for each touch |
| Test and spend | Smallest useful pilot, baseline, attribution limits, decision window, and stop or scale rule |
| Readiness | Verified destination, permissions, audience exclusions, response owner, and support plan |

Compare owned channels, external communities or partners, and paid reach individually; one may be enough. Adapt the same promise to each format instead of posting the same message everywhere. For partner or sales handoff, include approved claims, common objections, collateral, and which questions need an owner rather than an improvised answer.

Check the destination, tracking, disclosure, campaign dates, link visibility and approved terms using a preview or test recipient where authorized. Prepare the assets or calendar within scope; sending, publishing, outreach, and spend require their own authority.
