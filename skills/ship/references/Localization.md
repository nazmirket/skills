# Localization brief

Use when a regional launch changes who can use a product or how they complete a task. Identify the language, script and region, intended audience, supported platform, critical journey and launch depth. A waitlist test and a paid regional release need different evidence. Reuse the existing locale support policy. Keep currency, time zone, billing region and language as separate choices.

Trace one representative journey from discovery through completion and recovery. For each affected surface, record current behavior, the local need and the smallest change that meets it. Distinguish translation, adaptation, capability work and deliberate deferral. For a brief, identify which checks have evidence and who will run the rest. Use sanctioned fixtures when verifying transactions.

| Surface | Evidence for a decision |
| --- | --- |
| Content and interface | Give translators the string in context, variable meanings, protected names and length constraints. Exercise expansion, plural forms, missing translations and mixed text direction where relevant. Record which fallback a user actually sees. |
| Data and transactions | Run a representative address or name through entry, validation, storage and redisplay. Check dates, decimal separators, units, price, payment and receipt against the promised experience. |
| Offer and support | Verify eligibility, service coverage, support language and hours. Assign unresolved payment, legal or policy questions to accountable owners. Keep claims conditional until they answer. |
| Market-facing material | Identify examples, imagery, search terms and proof needing local judgment. Give approved terms, availability and claim boundaries to [Market](../../market/references/MessagingAndCopy.md#adapt-an-approved-message). |

For example, a visitor selects French to book a class in Montréal while travelling in another time zone. Check that the booking and confirmation agree on the venue's local time, that the price identifies the approved currency and that a cancellation reaches the promised support path. A fluent reviewer familiar with the intended market can verify meaning in context. Label any outstanding language review separately from observed layout and formatting checks.

Return a brief per locale with the launch slice, exclusions, changes, owners, acceptance cases and open decisions. A translated landing page with an unsupported checkout can support an expressly scoped interest test. Keep a paid launch pending until its purchase and recovery paths have evidence.
